It is hard to pick a favourite season. While many might take the transition between seasons for granted, in Malta we actually have fairly subtle differences between the seasons and almost no Spring and Autumn at all. On the other hand, in Budapest&nbsp;you can definitely know which season is approaching because you can clearly&nbsp;see the changes happening around you.
After the cold winter months, I have been&nbsp;looking forward to days starting getting longer and warmer. Spring brings back so much life and colour to nature. A walk around the city and hiking in the countryside lets us&nbsp;appreciate this.
