We also met some of our old friends to catch up over drinks, lunch or a bbq. Although we had planned to do a lot of stuff, time seemed too short to fit in everything we wanted to do.
All good things come to an end and as our trip to Malta came to an end earlier this week, I would like to share some of my favourite photos I took during our time on the Islands.
