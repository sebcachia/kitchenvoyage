There is a ritual in our kitchen: Saturday mornings, a sleepy playlist, and a batch of pancakes fluffy enough to make the neighbors jealous. These lemon ricotta beauties are Marica’s go-to—delicate, vibrant, and perfect with a drizzle of honey.

### Make it yours

- Swap honey for maple syrup if you prefer a deeper sweetness.
- Add a handful of poppy seeds for texture.
- Fold in a spoonful of lemon curd for an extra citrus burst.
