For most of us, Christmas is the season to eat and indulge. We tend to eat more than usual and when the new year starts, we are all trying to switch our lifestyle and keep away from the sweets and delicious stuff.
After some research and going through my existing trustworthy recipes, I came up with the below recipe for decadent chocolate muffins. Besides being very chocolatey and obviously super delicious, these muffins are light and airy and with added protein due to the addition of Greek yoghurt into the batter. I have also used a mixture of wholemeal and all purpose flour. If you prefer, you may also add some chocolate chips for extra texture and more chocolatiness.
These chocolate muffins are simple and easy to prepare and I am sure they will be enjoyed by everyone you share them with. I decided to decorate mine with a simple [white chocolate ganache](/recipes/madeira-cake-white-chocolate-ganache/) and dressed them for the festive season with homemade holly decorations. However, this is totally optional and can be decorated (or not!) according to your particular occasion.
