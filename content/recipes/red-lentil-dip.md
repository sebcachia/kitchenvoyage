How many times did you wish you had something healthy to snack on in between meals that was super delicious? Here is your new favourite!
I always try to plan my meals in advance to make sure that I do not end up snacking on food which is not that healthy. When I am at home for a couple of days in a row, I like to prepare some dips, which I can easily spread on my homemade bread or dip some crudités in.
One of my favourite snacks is this Red lentil dip. It is definitely a flavour explosion with a couple of powerful ingredients. It is very easy and simple to prepare.
