2 years ago today we celebrated a somewhat unusual Valentine's Day... It was the day we reached the summit of Kilimanjaro, the highest free standing mountain in the world.
After 8 days on the mountain and a cruel trek starting from our last campsite at 2am, we managed to reach Uhuru peak, all in good spirit. Even though it was tough, it was a beautiful experience, one which I will treasure forever.

On this day when the world celebrates love, I just want to share this quote with you:
"The day the power of love overrules the love of power, the world will know peace" - Mahatma Gandhi
Happy Valentines's Day everyone! &lt;3
