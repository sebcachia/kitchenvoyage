Budapest is a beautiful city, home to various architecture styles and famous for its art nouveau buildings. By day, you can admire the intricate details of these&nbsp;buildings and the majesty of various palaces. At night, all prominent buildings around the city are lit up and the spectacle they create is truly amazing.
The views by the river Danube are breathtaking any time of the day. Actually, a stroll in the evening along the river&nbsp;banks is a recommended romantic thing to do on various website when researching a trip to Budapest. Even though I have&nbsp;been here for the past 2 years, the sight&nbsp;still gives me chills to this day. It is really magnificent!
Being lucky enough to live within walking distance from the river banks, my evening walks very often take me towards that direction. On various occasions, I have tagged my camera along&nbsp;to capture these views. Recently, I went through my library of photos and decided to choose a couple and share them with you here. I hope you enjoy them as much as I enjoyed capturing them... and maybe also tempt&nbsp;you to pay a visit to this beautiful city! :)
