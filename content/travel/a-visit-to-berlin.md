Last week, we decided to take a short trip to Berlin for the weekend. Flights between Budapest and Berlin are conveniently scheduled on Saturdays early in the morning and return on Sundays late in evening.
They were definitely two long days. However, we managed to get around the city and explore quite a lot. We were very lucky with the weather and even got a bit sunburnt by Saturday evening!
Due to the lack of time, we did not enter any museums (besides we were glad to enjoy the nice weather while walking from one place to another!). Maybe it will be an excellent excuse to visit Berlin once again! :)
